# What's new

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-17

- Fetch, pull and push right from the toolbar, which shows how many commits are waiting to be pulled or pushed
- Rebase your branch onto another branch or tag, with a preview of the commits that will be replayed
- Get clear guidance when a pull, merge or rebase stops on conflicts, and continue, skip or abort with one click
- Repositories now fetch in the background, so you see new remote changes without asking
- Changed files are shown as a folder tree; collapse folders, or stage and unstage a whole folder at once
- A fresh layout with a toolbar on top and your repository tabs in their own bar

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-15

- Open several repositories side by side in tabs, and pick up where you left off on the next start
- Switch branches quickly with Ctrl/Cmd+B: search, check out remote branches or create a new one
- See changed images before and after a commit, right in the history
- Write commit messages in Markdown with a live preview, and read them formatted in the history
- Choose where your repositories live in the new Settings dialog
- On Linux, grit now shows up in the app search and the dock with its icon and description

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-14

- See your unstaged, staged and conflicted files with a diff for each, and stage or unstage them with a click
- Commit and amend, with Ctrl/Cmd+Enter to commit
- Browse the history with a branch graph, commit details and the changes in every commit
- See your branches, remotes and tags, and switch branches with a double-click
- Stays up to date automatically as files and branches change
- Light and dark themes, resizable panes, and your last repository reopens on start
